Roster · Spring 2026

Five painters,
one quiet circle.

The gallery represents a small, deliberately slow group of painters. Each artist applies for representation, signs the gallery's charter, and uploads their own works through the artist portal. We sell nothing we have not lived with.

No. 01 · Founding Artist

Asti Rahardjo

b. 1991, Yogyakarta · lives & works in Yogyakarta · oil on canvas

"Whoever I am painting, I am looking for. The work is the search — not its conclusion."

Rahardjo paints figures borrowed from the European canon and asks them to speak the languages they were never allowed to say. Her ongoing Finding Mainera series — from which the gallery takes its name — re-stages the fairy-tale vanity portrait as an act of self-summoning rather than self-recognition.

Recent solo presentations include The Country That Was Never Asked (ROH Projects, 2025) and Finding Mainera (Cemeti, 2023). Her work is held in private collections in Jakarta, Singapore, Tokyo and Berlin.

No. 02

Lien Hara

b. 1989, Jakarta · lives & works between Jakarta & Kyoto · oil on linen

"The interior is the only landscape I have ever been able to finish."

Hara's quiet domestic interiors are not nostalgic but slowly insistent — small rooms, repeated lamps, kitchen tables at six o'clock. She studied at IKJ Jakarta and Kyoto Seika; her work has been included in After the Fairy Tale (Yavuz, 2022) and Two Rooms, One Window (Cemeti, 2023).

No. 03

Jovan Aritonang

b. 1986, Medan · lives & works in Berlin · oil on canvas

"My maps are mostly margin. The country is where the hand rests."

Aritonang's large-format paintings sit between cartography and figuration. He treats the canvas as a contested territory — names, contours and outlines fail or are revised in real time. His work was included in the 2023 Singapore Biennale's Natasha programme.

No. 04

Nadira Khoury

b. 1993, Beirut · lives & works between Beirut & Jakarta · oil & text on canvas

"Some letters are addressed only after they have been answered."

Khoury writes a letter onto the canvas, then paints it back out — leaving only what the painting can bear to hold. Her practice sits at the intersection of contemporary painting and a quiet diasporic poetics. She is the inaugural recipient of the Yavuz Emerging Award (2023).

No. 05

Kei Sato

b. 1995, Tokyo · lives & works in Tokyo · oil on canvas

"At night, things take their proper size. Small."

Sato's Nocturne series — small-format oils painted only between midnight and dawn — proposes the night as the only honest condition for the still life. Five works from the series are currently on view. He joined the roster in early 2024.
