Rahardjo's Finding Mainera, No. I opens with a borrowed body — a 19th-century European figure lifted from the canon of vanity portraiture — and ends with a sentence the canon never permitted her to say.

The mirror, traditionally an instrument of self-recognition, is here re-coded as an instrument of self-summoning. The figure does not look into the mirror to confirm herself; she looks in to find a self that has not yet arrived. The redacted band laid across the eyes is, in this reading, less a censorship than a sentence the painting still owes us.

"Whoever I am painting, I am looking for. The work is the search, not its conclusion." — Asti Rahardjo, studio notes, December 2022

I began this painting on a morning that was loud with news. I had been thinking, for a long time, about the question Snow White's stepmother asks the mirror — and how, in our own language, the mirror has been asked the same thing, badly, and answered worse.

I wanted to paint a figure who asks the question but refuses the answer. I think this is the first time I have painted a self I would like to meet.

— A.R., Yogyakarta, December 2022

Provenance: The artist's studio, Yogyakarta. The work has not previously been exhibited publicly; it remained in the studio archive until selected for the inaugural Finding Mainera programme.

Exhibition history: Finding Mainera — Opening Programme, Yogyakarta & online, 2022 · Finding Mainera (solo), Cemeti Institute, 2023.

Literature: Khouw, T., "The Slow Question," Whiteboard Journal, Issue 41, 2023, pp. 88–93. · Aritonang, J., After the Fairy Tale, exh. cat., Yavuz Gallery, 2022, ill. p. 14.

Authentication: A signed certificate of authenticity from the artist accompanies the work, alongside high-resolution provenance documentation issued by Finding Mainera Gallery upon acquisition.

For the collector. This is the founding work of the series and of the gallery. There is a quiet weight to acquiring it — both as an art object and as a piece of cultural authorship. The work is unframed; we recommend a hand-finished walnut or oak floater, leaving a 6 mm reveal.

Lighting. The painting is built on warm umbers and muted greens; a 3000 K source at a 30° angle, dimmable, presents the surface most truthfully.

Insurance value (suggested). IDR 180.000.000 · ~ USD 11,000 (May 2026).
