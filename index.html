No. 001 / Featured Work

A self that
refuses to settle.

The work the gallery is named after. Asti Rahardjo, painting her way toward a figure she has not yet met.
Asti Rahardjo · Finding Mainera, No. I

"Whoever
I am painting,
I am looking for."

The opening work of Rahardjo's eponymous series — and the painting from which this gallery quietly took its name. A figure leans toward a hand-mirror; the surface returns not her face but a line of script — the painter's own, addressed to herself.

Painted in oil across a slow studio winter, the work establishes the visual grammar around which the gallery has since gathered: figuration borrowed from the European archive, then re-narrated in a language that is older, softer, and entirely its own.

No. 004 / Letter from the Gallery

On running a
gallery slowly.

Finding Mainera · Curatorial Note, 2026

The gallery is named after a painting — Asti Rahardjo's Finding Mainera, No. I — and after the disposition it asks of everyone who stands in front of it. We open our doors to painters who are still finding their figure, and to collectors who are willing to wait with them.

Every work on this site was uploaded by the artist, in their own hand. Bids are placed in public; the artist's reply is private. There are no algorithmic recommendations, no infinite scroll. The gallery's pace is its principal feature.

We believe in slow stewardship, fair pricing, and the long acquaintance between a painting and the home that finally takes it in.

— The Curator, Finding Mainera
